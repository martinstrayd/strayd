STRAYD — WORLD-CLASS WEBSITE BUILD V3
====================================

BRAND RULES
- Primary band / website brand: STRAYD (bold, clean, editorial typography).
- Duo campaign billing: SUZUKI & STRAYD.
- Martin’s individual artist name remains Martin Strayd.
- Suzuki is a manager, vocalist and band member; do not depict her playing guitar.
- Visual language: premium music-editorial, black / white / hot pink, cinematic photography. This is a website, not a flyer.

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a modern browser. It is a self-contained static site and works without a backend. Upload the complete folder to a static web host or the existing strayd.com hosting account. Preserve folder names and relative paths.

HERO VIDEO
Put a 10–20 second MP4 H.264 clip at videos/strayd-hero.mp4. It plays muted, loops and sits behind the homepage headline. Keep the important faces/action in the centre 60% of the frame; do not bake text into the video. Recommended export: 1920x1080, 16:9, 24/25/30 fps, target 8–20 Mbps, ideally under 25 MB. Use a still frame or the included duo image as fallback.

MAIN VIDEO
Put the official music video at videos/strayd-feature.mp4. Recommended 1920x1080, 16:9, H.264; for very long/high-resolution videos, use an official YouTube embed instead to avoid heavy hosting bandwidth.

WEEKLY RELEASE WORKFLOW
1. Open index.html and update the featured title, release copy, artwork and direct listening URL in the music section.
2. Update agent-info/music-data.json with the confirmed title, release date, official URLs and current availability.
3. Update llms.txt if core facts or links change.
4. Verify all links on desktop and mobile before publishing. Do not invent release dates, tour dates, streaming numbers or song titles.

STREAMING LINKS
The site includes Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, YouTube Music, Shazam, Amazon Music, Deezer, TIDAL, MusicBrainz, the existing STRAYD catalogue and sync licensing. Apple Music and Amazon Music use located artist pages; other streaming links are searches until the exact artist-profile URLs are verified. Replace those search destinations when you have the official URLs.

SOCIAL LINKS
TikTok, Instagram and Facebook discovery links are present, but they are not a substitute for verified official profile URLs. Replace with exact handles when confirmed.
